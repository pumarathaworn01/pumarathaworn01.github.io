Quantitative · Multi-market

Impact of COVID-19 on stock returns in ASEAN

Senior project at Maejo University (with Wanvilai Chulapan). I built a dataset across five markets and used OLS regression to test how deaths, testing and lockdowns moved sector returns in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand and Vietnam, Jan 2020 – Nov 2021.

Finding: services and industrial stocks fell most as deaths rose and lockdowns began, while wider testing lifted returns in every market.

User needs · Testing in stages

Tomorn trading system
