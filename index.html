Research

Impact of COVID-19 on Stock Returns in ASEAN

Senior project in International Economics at Maejo University (with Wanvilai Chulapan). Tested how COVID-19 deaths, testing and lockdowns moved sector stock returns in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand and Vietnam, Jan 2020 – Nov 2021.

Finding: services and industrial stocks fell most on rising deaths and lockdowns, while wider COVID-19 testing lifted returns in every market.
